The name

What connects galaxies.

A filament is the largest structure in the universe. A thread of dark matter and gas running across hundreds of millions of light-years. The threads came first; matter condensed at the junctions. The collection is named for that.

Each piece is a junction. The seed is the gravity. The render is what condenses. Nothing here was drawn by hand — a few rules, a number to start from, and the structure falls out.

Procedurally generated, like everything else in the universe.
